# Resume Forge: Weekly Content Engine Strategy

This document outlines the repeatable weekly content plan for Resume Forge to build massive semantic surface area and authority in the career tools niche.

## 1. THE REPEATABLE WEEKLY TEMPLATE SYSTEM

### Template A: Cluster Article (700-1000 words)
*Target: Daily educational depth and long-tail keyword dominance.*

- **H1:** `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`: Complete Guide for `[TARGET_AUDIENCE]` in 2026
- **AI Overview Block:** 30-word intro definition placed immediately after H1.
- **H2:** Why `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` Matters for `[AUDIENCE/USE_CASE]`
- **H2:** How to `[ACTION]` with `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` (Step-by-Step)
- **H2:** Top `[NUMBER]` `[STRATEGIES/TOOLS]` for `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`
- **H3:** Detailed breakdown of each (with real resume examples)
- **H2:** Common Mistakes to Avoid (including a comparison table)
- **AEO Box:** 5 Key Takeaways (bulleted list)
- **AEO Box:** FAQ Section (5 questions) + FAQPage Schema
- **Internal Links:** 1 Pillar Page + 2 related Cluster Articles + `/builder`

### Template B: Programmatic Comparison Page (500-800 words)
*Target: High-intent "vs" keywords and competitor comparisons.*

- **H1:** `[ENTITY_A]` vs `[ENTITY_B]`: Which Resume Builder is Better for `[USE_CASE]`?
- **Quick Verdict:** 1-sentence AI summary (e.g., "Resume Forge wins for ATS-safe free exports, while Canva leads in visual flair.")
- **Comparison Table:** (Columns: Features, `[ENTITY_A]`, `[ENTITY_B]`, Winner)
- **Deep Dive [ENTITY_A]:** Pros & Cons
- **Deep Dive [ENTITY_B]:** Pros & Cons
- **Scenario Guide:** When to choose each.
- **AEO Box:** "Best Pick for [Scenario]"
- **Internal Links:** Related Pillar + `/builder`

### Template C: Micro-Answer Page (300-500 words)
*Target: Featured snippets, voice search, and AEO "Quick Answer" blocks.*

- **H1:** What is `[RESUME_TERM]`? (Direct question format)
- **AI Snapshot:** 40-60 word precise definition.
- **H2:** How Does `[TERM]` Work?
- **H2:** Real-World Example of `[TERM]` in `[INDUSTRY]`
- **H2:** Why is `[TERM]` Important?
- **H2:** `[TERM]` vs `[RELATED_TERM]` (Comparison table)
- **AEO Box:** Core Principles (bulleted list)
- **Internal Links:** 1 related Cluster + `/builder`

### Template D: Pillar Page (3000-7000 words)
*Target: Main topic authority and internal linking hub.*

- **H1:** The Ultimate 2026 Guide to `[BROAD_TOPIC]`
- **Executive Summary:** 200-word overview for AI Overviews.
- **Navigation:** Table of Contents with jump links.
- **Chapters:**
    - Introduction & Evolution (History of the topic)
    - Technical Foundation (How it works under the hood)
    - Top 10 Strategies for 2026
    - Case Study / Real-World Implementation
    - Future Trends (Post-2026 outlook)
- **AEO Box:** Statistical Data Table (e.g., Industry trends, adoption rates)
- **AEO Box:** 10+ FAQ Section with full Schema.org markup.
- **Internal Links:** 15+ related Cluster Articles + `/builder`

### Template E: Industry/Job Title Page (600-800 words)
*Target: Commercial intent for specific professions.*

- **H1:** `[JOB_TITLE]` Resume Template: Complete 2026 Guide
- **Section:** What recruiters look for in `[JOB_TITLE]` resumes.
- **Section:** Key skills and keywords for `[JOB_TITLE]`.
- **Section:** Recommended resume format for `[JOB_TITLE]` (e.g., Chronological vs Hybrid).
- **Section:** Sample bullet points and achievements using the STAR method.
- **AEO Box:** Quick Reference Table (Skill → Keyword → Achievement Example)
- **Internal Links:** Industry Pillar + `/builder` + Top 3 City Guides

### Template F: Location-Specific Guide (500-700 words)
*Target: Local SEO dominance for 50+ US cities.*

- **H1:** `[CITY]` Resume Guide: Stand Out in the `[CITY]` Job Market
- **Section:** Top industries and major employers in `[CITY]`.
- **Section:** Local hiring standards and cultural preferences.
- **Section:** Salary expectations for `[CITY]` (2026 data).
- **Section:** Best local job boards and career resources.
- **AEO Box:** Quick Stats Table (Industry → Avg Salary → Top Employers)
- **Internal Links:** General Location Pillar + `/builder` + Industry Page
